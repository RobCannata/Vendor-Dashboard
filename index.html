Vendor scorecard weighting

Approved five-dimension weighting from the Vendor Scorecard list.

100% total
Weight source: Vendor Scorecard list. Only the five approved dimensions are used: Execution 25%, Quality 30%, Communication 20%, Compliance & Safety 15%, and Reliability 10%. For 1–5 category ratings, the weighted score is calculated as the sum of each rating divided by 5 and multiplied by its assigned weight. Project and vendor percentages displayed in the dashboard remain the recorded final scorecard values where available; the dashboard does not substitute unsupported category ratings. Tracker status and vendor cost are calculated from the filtered January–August 3 records. Revenue and profit remain modeled until customer billing data is added.
